Install Arc TV on Android

The Arc TV app for Android phones and tablets is installed from the latest release file (an APK). It takes about two minutes.

What you need

  • An Android phone or tablet
  • An internet connection

Step by step

  1. Download the app

    On your phone or tablet, open the latest Arc TV APK. If your browser warns that this type of file can be harmful, choose Download anyway.

  2. Open the file

    When the download finishes, open it from the notification or from your Downloads folder.

  3. Allow the install

    If Android says your phone isn’t allowed to install unknown apps from this source, choose Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.

  4. Install and sign in

    Select Install, then Open. Sign in with your Arc TV account to pick up your list and progress.

Updating

To update Arc TV, download the latest APK again and install it over the top. Your account and settings are kept.

Troubleshooting

The install is blocked

Repeat step 3 for the app you used to open the file, such as your browser or file manager.

I use an iPhone or iPad

There’s no iPhone or iPad app yet. Open the web app in your browser instead.

Arc TV doesn’t host, store or provide any media. Use it only with content you have the right to access.
