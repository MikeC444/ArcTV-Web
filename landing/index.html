Questions.

What is Arc TV?

Arc TV is a modern media centre for movies and TV shows. It runs on the web and on Fire TV and Android TV, all with the same account.

How does it work?

Open the web app and start browsing. Create an account when you press Play or save a title, then install the add-ons you want. After that, explore Movies, TV Shows, Genres and Search.

Do I need an account?

Not to browse. An account is needed to play, keep My List and Continue Watching, and sync between devices.

Can I add content to Arc TV?

Arc TV doesn’t provide content itself. The more add-ons you install, the more you can find.

Is my data the same on web and TV?

Yes. The web app is just another device on your account, so My List, Continue Watching, add-ons and settings are shared.

What Arc TV is not.

Arc TV is a client-side interface. It browses metadata and plays media supplied by add-ons you install or sources you provide, and is intended for content you own or are otherwise authorised to access. It does not host, store or distribute media.
