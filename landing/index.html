Everything in one place

Browse, pick a source, press play. Arc TV keeps the rest in sync for you.

  • One account, every screen

    Sign in on the web or your Fire TV and see the same My List, Continue Watching and settings.

  • Pick up where you left off

    Progress is saved as you watch, and the next episode starts on its own with a countdown.

  • Add-ons, your way

    Install add-ons by URL to bring in more catalogues and sources. Choose your own Home rows and their order.

  • Picked for you

    Suggestions based on what you like, split by genre — and you can remove anything that isn’t for you.

  • Choose your source

    See quality, size and health for each source, with a recommended pick at the top.

  • A real player

    Pick audio and subtitle tracks, change speed and quality, seek with previews, and go fullscreen.

Get the Fire TV app

Up and running in a minute

  1. Open the web app. Browse straight away — no sign-up needed.
  2. Create your account. It only asks when you press Play or save a title.
  3. Sign in on your TV. Use the same email, or scan the QR code. Everything is already there.
