FAQ

What is Arc TV?

Arc TV is a modern media center for movies and TV shows. It works on the web and on Fire TV and Android TV, all with the same account.

How does it work?

Open the web app and start browsing. Create an account when you press Play or save a title, then install any add-ons you want. After that, explore Movies, TV Shows, Genres and Search.

Do I need an account?

Not to browse. You can look around the web app as a guest. An account is needed to play, keep My List and Continue Watching, and sync across devices.

What devices does Arc TV support?

Any modern web browser, plus Amazon Fire TV and Android TV through the Arc TV app.

Can I add content to Arc TV?

Arc TV doesn’t provide content itself. The more add-ons you install, the more content you can find.

Is my data the same on web and TV?

Yes. The web app is just another device on your account, so My List, Continue Watching, add-ons and settings are shared.
